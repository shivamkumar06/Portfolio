# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: hiring managers and recruiters evaluating Shivam Kumar for software / full-stack roles. Secondary: peers and followers who open the site from social media shares and need a strong first-viewport impression.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that showcases Shivam’s highlights-projects, skills, background, and resume-so visitors can decide to contact, hire, or share. Success means a hiring manager can reach proof (projects) and the CV in one or two clicks, and a shared link still reads as a distinctive personal brand on first view.

## Positioning

Shivam presents as a software developer (GFK-NielsenIQ) who works across high-performance backends and React frontends-JavaScript/TypeScript/Node on the product surface, with Java/Spring Boot and published technical writing in the proof set. Neighboring generic “dev portfolio” templates cannot truthfully claim this specific role, company, project set, or publication.

## Operating Context

Visited on desktop and mobile browsers; often opened from LinkedIn, GitHub, Twitter/X, Instagram, or a direct resume link. Deployed as a static SPA on Netlify. Content is owned and updated by Shivam.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Routes: Home (`/`), About (`/about`), Projects (`/project`), Resume (`/resume`).
- Features to preserve: preloader, sticky nav, typewriter role strings, home intro + avatar, project cards with GitHub/demo links, tech and tool icon grids, GitHub contribution calendar, in-browser PDF resume + download, social links, Netlify SPA redirects.
- Assets to preserve: existing images and `RESUME_SHIVAM_KUMAR_NOV.pdf` under `src/Assets/`.
- Redesign may replace visual chrome and tooling (Vite, no Bootstrap/particles) but must not invent new projects, testimonials, metrics, or bio claims.
- Stack target: Vite + React (modernization from CRA).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Shivam Kumar (site title “Shivam | Portfolio”).
- Existing logo (`logo.png`) and illustration/photo assets remain.
- Voice in existing copy: direct, engineer-forward; quote “Strive to build things that make a difference!”

## Evidence on Hand

- Bio facts: Software Developer at GFK-NielsenIQ; from Pune, India; Master’s in Computer Applications, VIT Vellore.
- Projects: Reet Sangeet, MovieBox, Foodie App, blockchain book chapter (Apple Academic Press, 2023).
- Resume PDF: `src/Assets/RESUME_SHIVAM_KUMAR_NOV.pdf`.
- Socials: GitHub `shivamkumar06`, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram (URLs in site).
- Do not fabricate reviews, employers, or additional case studies.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over ornament - projects and resume stay easy to find.
2. Same facts, new craft - redesign the look without changing truth.
3. Shareable first screen, hireable next click.
4. Keep real assets; do not substitute stock for Shivam’s images.
5. Performance and accessibility matter on a hiring evaluation path.
